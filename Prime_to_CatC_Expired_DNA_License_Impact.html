The Paradigm Shift: Cisco Prime Infrastructure to Catalyst Center

Moving from reactive device-by-device SNMP/CLI to centralized intent-based network automation & AI assurance

Cisco Prime Infrastructure has reached its End-of-Life (EoL) milestone. Enterprise networks running modern Catalyst 9000 Series switching, Catalyst 9800 Series wireless controllers, and Wi-Fi 6E/7 access points require Cisco Catalyst Center (formerly DNA Center) as their central management and observability engine.

Legacy Architecture

Cisco Prime Infrastructure

  • Management Protocol: SNMP polling, CLI scraping, Syslog traps (high overhead, slow sync).
  • Configuration Model: Template-based scripts, manual CLI overrides per device.
  • Visibility: Static device inventory, basic interface up/down status, delayed polling cycles.
  • Troubleshooting: Reactive alert storms; manual packet captures and CLI debugs required.
  • License Model: Node-based perpetual device licenses (tied to hardware).
→
Modern Intent-Based

Cisco Catalyst Center (CatC)

  • Management Protocol: Model-driven telemetry, gRPC streaming, NETCONF/YANG automation.
  • Configuration Model: Centralized intent-based policies, golden image SWIM, Zero-Touch Provisioning (PnP).
  • Visibility: Real-time streaming telemetry, Client 360, Device 360, Application 360 observability.
  • Troubleshooting: AI Network Analytics, Machine Reasoning Engine (MRE), automated root-cause isolation.
  • License Model: Tiered Subscription (Cisco DNA Essentials / Advantage / Premier).
Migration Incentive & Investment Protection

Cisco offers credit programs and migration toolkits (Prime Data Migration Tool / PDMT) that allow existing Prime customers to migrate network topologies, templates, maps, and device credentials directly into Catalyst Center with zero loss of site metadata.

Cisco Catalyst Switching & Wireless License Structure

Understanding the two distinct license layers: Perpetual Network Stack vs. Term-Based Software Subscription

Every Cisco Catalyst 9000 switch and access point purchase includes two co-existing software license layers. It is vital for customer executives and engineering leads to understand what each layer governs:

1. Perpetual Hardware License Never Expires

Network Essentials / Network Advantage

  • Tied permanently to the switch / AP hardware serial number.
  • Governs all Layer 2 switching, Layer 3 routing (OSPF, BGP, EIGRP, VRF), and physical hardware forwarding.
  • Includes High Availability (StackWise-160/480/1T, StackWise Virtual).
  • Security ACLs, standard QoS, 802.1X port security.
  • Does NOT expire—even if subscription terms end, network packet switching continues uninterrupted.
2. Term-Based Software Subscription 3, 5, or 7-Year Term

Cisco DNA Essentials / Advantage / Premier

  • Enables advanced automation, AI analytics, and policy in Catalyst Center.
  • DNA Essentials: Basic Discovery, Topology, Software Image Management (SWIM), Network Assurance.
  • DNA Advantage: SD-Access Fabric, Cisco ISE Integration, AI Network Analytics, Machine Reasoning Engine (MRE), ThousandEyes Enterprise Agents.
  • DNA Premier: Includes Cisco ISE Base/Plus, Stealthwatch / Secure Network Analytics licenses.

What Happens When DNA / Catalyst Software Licenses Expire?

Detailed functional, operational, and security impact breakdown across network operations
Key Architectural Reality: Switching & Forwarding Remain Operational

There is NO data plane outage or packet drop when a Cisco DNA subscription expires. Physical hardware forwarding, spanning-tree, routing protocols, and active client traffic remain 100% operational under the perpetual Network Essentials/Advantage license. However, advanced management, AI assurance, and automation capabilities inside Catalyst Center will degrade or disable.

⚠

Assurance & AI Telemetry

Impact: Disabled / Degraded. Loss of Client 360, Device 360, Machine Reasoning Engine (MRE), Wi-Fi RF heatmaps, and predictive AI Network Analytics. Granular health scores and historical telemetry retention are disabled.

⚠

Automated Provisioning & SWIM

Impact: Restricted. Software Image Management (SWIM), automated golden image compliance checking, Day-N template pushes, and Zero-Touch Plug-and-Play (PnP) are blocked for non-compliant devices.

⚠

SD-Access & Security Policy

Impact: Frozen / Read-Only. Existing Scalable Group Tags (SGTs) and fabric encapsulation in hardware continue to forward traffic, but new fabric provisioning, dynamic segmentation updates, and Cisco ISE TrustSec matrix synchronization halt.

⚠

Catalyst Center Compliance Status

Impact: Out-of-Compliance Alert. Catalyst Center flags expired devices with red "Out of Compliance" banners in Smart Software Manager (SSM) and blocks batch policy enforcement.

⚠

Third-Party Integrations & APIs

Impact: Throttled. ITSM integrations (ServiceNow CMDB sync), ThousandEyes Enterprise Agent monitoring, and REST API telemetry streams requiring Advantage tiers are disabled.

⚠

TAC Support Entitlements

Impact: Gated Software Support. Cisco TAC provides hardware replacement under Smart Net Total Care, but Catalyst Center software application patches and major feature upgrades require active software support.

CSM Strategic Remediation & Renewal Playbook

Actionable guidance for Customer Success Managers to mitigate renewal risk and restore full value
✔
Step 1: Conduct Smart Account & Virtual Account (SA/VA) License Audit
Access Cisco Smart Software Manager (CSSM) and cross-reference device counts against active entitlements. Identify all Cat9K switches and APs nearing or past expiration dates.
✔
Step 2: Leverage Smart Licensing with Policy (SLP) & Evaluation Licenses
For critical customer escalations, provision temporary 90-day Cisco Catalyst evaluation licenses directly in Catalyst Center or via Cisco TAC to restore full AI Assurance and SWIM capabilities while contract renewal paperwork is routed.
✔
Step 3: Position Cisco Enterprise Agreement (EA 3.0) Networking Enrollment
Consolidate fragmented 3-year point subscriptions across campus switches, data center fabrics, and wireless APs into a unified, co-terminated Cisco Enterprise Agreement (EA 3.0) with True-Forward growth allowances.
✔
Step 4: Demonstrate Realized Value via Quarterly Success Reviews (QSR)
Showcase operational metrics captured before expiration: Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) reduction, automated compliance audit savings, and ThousandEyes digital experience visibility.
Executive Presentation Summary

When presenting to customer CIOs / IT Directors, reassure them that packet forwarding and business operations are completely secure, while clearly articulating that renewing DNA Advantage subscriptions restores the automation, predictive intelligence, and zero-trust security required to keep modern enterprise networks resilient.
