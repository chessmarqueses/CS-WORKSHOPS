Workshop overview

Two connected days: ACI operations, then Nexus Dashboard

The agenda is scheduled for 09:00–17:00 PHT, with registration / networking from 08:30, lunch 12:00–13:00, and two coffee breaks each day. Format: face-to-face, presentation plus presenter-led demos; no attendee hands-on lab. Language: English.

Day 1 focus: ACI architecture, release and lifecycle positioning, upgrades, proactive health checks, automation, and operational pain points.

Day 2 focus: Nexus Dashboard 4.x, deployment and onboarding, Insights, Multi-Site federation, integrations, cluster lifecycle, and organization-level next steps.

Delivery organization: Cisco CX / Customer Success – Data Center Networking.

Tracker speaker: John Cui. Speaker assignments are not populated in the agenda workbook.

Audience: Data center network operations and architects from multiple Philippine organizations.
